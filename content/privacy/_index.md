# Privacy Notice for DataSaturday Oslo

## Introduction

DataSaturday Oslo respects your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you register for our conference, attend the event and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

## Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about attendees of our conference, including:

- *Personal Information*: Your name, email address, phone number, and other contact information.
- *Professional Information*: Your job title, employer, and professional interests.
- *Attendance Information*: If you attend our event or not.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect about you or that you provide to us, including any personal information:

- To provide you with information about the conference.
- To provide you with information about future events and updates.
- To improve our conference and better understand our attendees' needs.

## Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your personally identifiable information unless we provide you with advance notice.

However, if you do decide to scan your badge to participate in a sponsor’s raffle the sponsor gets access to your name and email address.

## Your Choices About Our Collection, Use, and Disclosure of Your Information

You can choose to not participate in the raffle.
You can choose to unsubscribe to notifications about future events.

## Changes to Our Privacy Policy

It is our policy to post any changes we make to our privacy policy on this page.

## Contact Information

To ask questions or comment about this privacy policy and our privacy practices, contact us at: contactus@datasaturday.no
