[Devart](https://www.devart.com/) is a global leader in data management, connectivity, and integration solutions. Our mission is simple: enable customers to manage data easily and effectively at an affordable cost. Over the past 29 years, we’ve built an ecosystem of database solutions. Today, more than 500,000 users, including 65% of Fortune 100 companies, rely on our products.  
At the core of our portfolio is [dbForge](https://www.devart.com/dbforge/), an AI-powered, unified ecosystem designed to streamline the entire database lifecycle across the most popular database systems and cloud platforms. It combines comprehensive DBMS-specific IDEs, standalone tools, add-ins for SSMS and Visual Studio, and multidatabase solutions.
